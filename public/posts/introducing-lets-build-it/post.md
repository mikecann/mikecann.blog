For the past year or so I've been building an app with my son Josh, and it's finally out. It's called [Let's Build It](https://letsbuildit.app) and it lets kids make their own apps and games just by talking.

The best way to get a feel for it is to watch the video Josh and I made. It explains everything from a high level:

<iframe width="100%" height="400" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/f3lNX8GmxZI" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe>

## Why I built it

Josh is 6 and an absolute legend, but he can't read yet. He's always got plenty of ideas though, and for a while I was the one typing them into Claude or Codex for him every single time. I wanted him to get that joy of building things himself, so Let's Build It is entirely voice driven. He just talks to it, and a minute or so later he's playing whatever he dreamt up.

I'd also much rather he spent his screen time being creative than passively watching stuff on his iPad. The problem is, a lot of the places kids make games have pretty perverse incentives, so there are no ads in Let's Build It and everything he makes stays private. I can also set up his profile so I control the kinds of things he builds.

## Try it out

It's free to download on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6775646688) for iPhone and iPad, and I'm actively working on the Android version. You can find out more on the [Let's Build It website](https://letsbuildit.app).

If you've got kids, I'd love to hear what they come up with!
